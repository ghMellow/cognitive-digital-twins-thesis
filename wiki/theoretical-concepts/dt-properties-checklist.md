A checklist of the 12 Digital Twin (DT) properties consolidated by Pretel et al. (2024), used to position implementations and distinguish DTs from digital shadows.

## The 12 properties (DT1–DT12)
- **DT1 Representativeness** — fidelity of digital representation
- **DT2 Reflection** — real-time synchronization
- **DT3 Entanglement** — bidirectional influence (actions affect the physical)
- **DT4 Identity** — unique identification of the entity
- **DT5 Memory** — historical persistence
- **DT6 Augmentation** — adding inferred/derived attributes
- **DT7 Autonomy** — self-decision/self-healing capability
- **DT8 Accountability** — traceability and auditability of decisions
- **DT9 Fidelity** — accuracy of predictions/simulations
- **DT10 Composability** — integration into larger systems
- **DT11 Interoperability** — standard interfaces and data exchange
- **DT12 Predictability** — reliable forecasting capability

## How this thesis uses it
- As a **Related Work positioning tool** to argue that many works implement digital shadows, while this thesis targets strong entanglement (DT3) and accountability (DT8).
