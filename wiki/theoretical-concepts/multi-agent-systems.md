A Multi-Agent System (MAS) is a set of specialized autonomous agents that coordinate to achieve a shared goal through structured interaction (communication, negotiation, task allocation).

## Why MAS for CDTs
Digital Twins provide synchronized state and simulation; MAS provides:
- Division of labor across cognitive functions
- Coordination and robustness via redundancy
- Explicit control-flow and verification points

## Role in this thesis
- Architecture: Perception → Reasoning → Planning → Communication as specialized agents.
- Evaluation: separate agent-level metrics from coordination-level metrics.
