## 🎯 Validation Workflow

**For each synthesis:**

1. **Author Review** — Mark any clarifications needed before presenting
2. **Present to Advisors** — Use "Points to Validate" section as agenda
3. **Collect Feedback** — Record in "Feedback ricevuti"
4. **Revise** — Update synthesis based on feedback
5. **Promote** — Once validated, move validated insights to canonical wiki pages
6. **Log Promotion** — Update status to `validated` and log the promotion

---

## 📊 Synthesis Evaluation Scorecard

After advisor feedback, each synthesis is evaluated on:

- **Theoretical soundness** — Are the references correct and properly applied?
- **Architectural coherence** — Does it fit the overall CDT design without contradictions?
- **Novelty claim** — Does it represent original contribution or restatement of literature?
- **Implementation feasibility** — Can it actually be built in the thesis timeline?
- **Evaluation tractability** — Can it be measured/validated empirically?
